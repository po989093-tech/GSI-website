Environmental Conservation

Healthy communities depend on healthy environments. Through our Environmental Conservation programme, GSI promotes practical actions that encourage communities and young people to take responsibility for their surrounding environment.

Overview

We strengthen environmental awareness and create opportunities for people to participate directly in conservation activities, turning awareness into practical action through community clean-ups, waste awareness and protection of green spaces.

Objectives

  • Promote responsible environmental practices within communities.
  • Increase awareness of pollution and waste-related challenges.
  • Encourage responsible waste management and reduction of littering.
  • Promote protection of green spaces and natural resources.
  • Encourage youth participation in conservation.

Key Activities

  • Community clean-up campaigns.
  • Waste management awareness activities.
  • Environmental awareness campaigns.
  • Green-space conservation activities.
  • Youth-led conservation initiatives.
