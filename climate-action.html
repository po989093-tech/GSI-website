Climate Action

Climate change is increasingly affecting communities through changing rainfall patterns, flooding, rising temperatures, environmental degradation and other climate-related challenges. Through our Climate Action programme, GSI works to strengthen climate awareness, community participation and practical action, particularly among young people.

Overview

We create opportunities for young people and communities to understand climate risks and explore practical ways of responding to them. Our approach connects climate education with action, helping communities understand how global climate challenges relate to local issues. We support awareness, engagement and practical environmental activities that build climate-resilient communities.

Objectives

  • Increase awareness and understanding of climate change and its local impacts.
  • Encourage young people to participate in practical climate action.
  • Promote community-based approaches to climate resilience and adaptation.
  • Connect climate education with practical environmental action.
  • Encourage collaboration among young people, communities and stakeholders.
  • Promote locally relevant solutions to climate-related challenges.

Key Activities

  • Climate change awareness campaigns.
  • Community climate education sessions.
  • Youth climate dialogues and discussions.
  • Tree planting and nature-based activities.
  • Community clean-up activities.
  • Climate resilience and adaptation awareness.
