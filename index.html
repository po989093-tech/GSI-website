Building Resilient Communities. Protecting Our Environment.

The Green Security Initiative (GSI) is a proposed youth-led climate resilience and environmental sustainability programme under Communities for Africa Network Kenya (CAN-K), aligned with the Sustainable Livelihoods & Economic Empowerment programme area. GSI is designed to strengthen CAN-K's commitment to sustainable community development by promoting climate action, environmental conservation, ecosystem restoration, green livelihoods, and youth leadership.

About GSI

The Green Security Initiative (GSI) is a proposed youth-led climate resilience and environmental sustainability programme under Communities for Africa Network Kenya (CAN-K), aligned with the Sustainable Livelihoods & Economic Empowerment programme area. GSI is designed to strengthen CAN-K's commitment to sustainable community development by promoting climate action, environmental conservation, ecosystem restoration, green livelihoods, and youth leadership.
