Green Livelihoods

GSI's Green Livelihoods programme promotes opportunities that connect environmental responsibility with sustainable economic participation for young people and communities.

Overview

We encourage young people to explore green entrepreneurship, sustainable practices and livelihood opportunities that contribute to community wellbeing while protecting the environment.

Objectives

  • Increase awareness of green economy opportunities.
  • Encourage youth interest in green entrepreneurship.
  • Promote environmentally responsible economic practices.
  • Support innovation around local environmental challenges.

Key Activities

  • Green entrepreneurship awareness sessions.
  • Green economy and livelihood workshops.
  • Training on sustainable practices.
  • Networking with environmental and livelihood stakeholders.
