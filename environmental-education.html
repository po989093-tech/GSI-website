Environmental Education

Knowledge is an important foundation for environmental action. GSI's Environmental Education programme creates practical learning opportunities that connect knowledge with local action.

Overview

We provide climate and environmental education that is practical and accessible, helping learners and communities translate knowledge into everyday action and stewardship.

Objectives

  • Increase environmental and climate literacy among young people and communities.
  • Make environmental knowledge accessible and practical.
  • Encourage people to connect learning with everyday action.
  • Promote understanding of waste management, conservation and resilience.

Key Activities

  • Environmental awareness sessions.
  • Climate education workshops.
  • School and youth environmental programmes.
  • Community outreach and practical learning.

Green Security Initiative (GSI)

A youth-led initiative advancing environmental security, climate resilience, and community action in Kenya.
