OUR PROGRAMMES

The Green Security Initiative delivers youth-led programmes that promote climate resilience, environmental conservation, ecosystem restoration, green livelihoods, and environmental education. Programmes are implemented through community engagement, capacity building, strategic partnerships, and locally driven action.

Climate Action

Strengthening community awareness and practical responses to climate impacts through youth-led actions, education and local adaptation activities.
