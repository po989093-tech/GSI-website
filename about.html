Who We Are

The Green Security Initiative (GSI) is a proposed youth-led climate resilience and environmental sustainability programme under Communities for Africa Network Kenya (CAN-K), aligned with the Sustainable Livelihoods & Economic Empowerment programme area. GSI is designed to strengthen CAN-K's commitment to sustainable community development by promoting climate action, environmental conservation, ecosystem restoration, green livelihoods, and youth leadership. Through community engagement, strategic partnerships, innovation, and environmental education, the initiative seeks to empower communities to address climate and environmental challenges while building resilient, inclusive, and sustainable societies.

Recognizing that environmental sustainability is closely linked to human well-being, economic opportunity, food security, water security, and community resilience, GSI adopts an integrated approach that combines conservation with practical, community-driven solutions to create lasting environmental and social impact.

Our Mission

To empower communities through climate action, environmental conservation, youth leadership, innovation, and sustainable livelihoods that strengthen environmental security and improve human well-being.

Our Vision

A resilient Africa where people, livelihoods, and ecosystems thrive together through climate resilience, environmental stewardship, and sustainable development.

Motto: Protecting Nature. Securing Our Future.

Our Approach

GSI works through community engagement, environmental education, youth leadership, strategic partnerships, innovation, and practical climate action. The initiative combines public awareness campaigns, school outreach, community training, and locally driven conservation and restoration activities to build resilient communities. GSI supports green livelihoods and entrepreneurship while promoting research, advocacy, and knowledge sharing to strengthen policy and practice at community and institutional levels.

Community Support

Volunteers

Volunteers are an important part of the Green Security Initiative. They contribute their time, skills, ideas and energy to support environmental action and strengthen communities. GSI welcomes young people and other individuals who are passionate about environmental conservation, climate action, ecosystem restoration, environmental education, community engagement and sustainable development to take part in our activities.

Volunteers can support community clean-ups, tree planting and ecosystem restoration activities, awareness campaigns, educational sessions, events, research, communications, outreach and other initiatives depending on their interests and skills.

What Volunteers Do

Volunteers can participate in GSI's environmental and community activities, including climate action, environmental conservation, ecosystem restoration, tree planting, community clean-ups, environmental education, awareness campaigns, events, outreach, communications, research and other initiatives.

Who Can Volunteer

Volunteering should be open to people who are interested in supporting GSI's mission and are willing to contribute their time, skills or ideas. Students, young professionals, community members, environmental enthusiasts and people with relevant skills can all contribute in different ways.

Areas of Contribution

  • Environmental conservation
  • Climate action
  • Ecosystem restoration
  • Environmental education
  • Community outreach
  • Youth leadership
  • Communications and social media
  • Photography and media
  • Research and advocacy
  • Event support
  • Project activities

What Volunteers Can Gain

Volunteering provides opportunities to gain practical experience, develop leadership and teamwork skills, improve communication and community engagement, learn about environmental and climate issues, meet other young people and changemakers, and contribute to positive environmental and social change.

Our Expectations

Volunteers should be willing to participate responsibly, respect communities and fellow volunteers, follow activity guidelines, work collaboratively and contribute positively to GSI activities. Volunteering is based on active participation, commitment and a willingness to learn.
